Language Tone

Click to insert: {greeting}{hook}{closing}{first_name}{last_name}{company}{one_liner}{job_title}{position}{city}{my_name}{role}{phone}{linkedin}{github}{portfolio}{cv}{location}{start_date}{salary}{work_permit}

{greeting} adapts to language, tone and recipient (Sehr geehrte Frau Dr. Weber, · Hallo Jonas, · Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren, for an inbox). {hook} mentions the job you found or the company's one-liner. {position} is the job title, or your target role. Any line whose placeholder is empty is dropped, so Gehaltsvorstellung: {salary} disappears if you leave salary blank.

Email labels: verified unverified from Apify (step 4) · on site personal address on the company's site or Impressum · in job ad · pattern built from a real address on the same domain · guess most common German pattern, may bounce · jobs inbox e.g. bewerbung@ · generic info@.

Before you send (Germany)
  • Use this for job applications only, one email per person. Pitching freelance or agency services by cold email counts as advertising under UWG § 7 and needs prior consent.
  • Founder and director emails are personal data (GDPR). Keep the list to yourself, don't build mailing lists, and stop when someone says no.
  • Formal Sie is the safe default for the Mittelstand. Many Berlin startups are fine with du or English, so check their careers page.
  • Edit the [bracketed] lines. A specific sentence about the company beats a long CV summary. Attach or link a PDF CV.
  • guess addresses can bounce. Send a few, watch for bounces, and switch to the jobs inbox when unsure.

4. Find verified emails (optional)

Without this you get emails from the company's site and Impressum plus pattern guesses. For verified addresses, paste your own Apify API token. Lookups run from your browser straight to Apify on your account. The token is never sent to this tool's server and isn't saved.
